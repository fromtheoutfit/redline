In `workflows/redline-loop.js`, `resolveWith` drops a deferred entry the resolve agent returns beside a decision that does not defer its finding: a disposition override to `fix` or `accept` (#103, R2-8) and, since #99, a judgment call whose decision the resolve agent stated as `fix` or `accept`.
