## Scope

On every round the built-in `code-review` skill is the **finder** and Redline's reviewer the **verifier**: the skill, run from a plain workflow agent over the whole pull request at the round's head, returns candidates; the reviewer applies its own contract to each (collapse by cause, the known-finding check, reproduction for correctness, a line inside the diff, category, severity, confidence, `needsDecision`, `askRule`, and two guards: outside the diff is never a finding, disproportionate is dropped with that word) and sends the rest to a new `### For the person` section in the review body and the handoff comment. On a later round the reviewer still rechecks the previous round's fix diff; the final nit pass is unchanged. The run finishes when a round's verifier confirms no new correctness finding, under `maxRounds`. A `coverage off` word, or `models.coverage: null` in `redline.json`, runs today's reviewer-only rounds under today's done rule as the fallback. Whether the reviewer's independent whole-PR pass stays beside the skill's (the union) or only behind the dial (verify-only) was decided by a second corpus PR, Task 2, before the code is built: verify-only.

In scope, from the issue: tasks 1 to 6 (the corpus PR, the coverage agent, the verify stage, `### For the person`, the dial and docs, the drift line), #73's items 3 and 4 (the "for the person" list and the reworded precision sentence). Out of scope: #73's regression pass and `cost` class, #74 (review-only at coverage) and #75 (the shaper's class check).

## Decision

**Verify-only.** The coverage pass is the finder and the reviewer the verifier, on every round that runs a coverage pass (Decision 20); the reviewer's independent discovery pass over the whole PR exists only behind `coverage off`. Tasks 4 to 10 are built as written, without their Union variant paragraphs.

Evidence, from `redline-internal/docs/SPIKE-72-NOTES.md` §3 to §5 (#817 at `da44437`, PR #818) and §9 (#778 at `db3eedbe2`, PR #819), both at `claude-opus-5-5` / `xhigh` with every finding reported (`--max-findings all`, Decision 4): on both PRs the reviewer's independent pass added no finding the skill's pass lacked by cause (three of three on #817; four of four on #778, three at the same line and one, the sheet's pick-close re-read, as the same cause described as a race), and the skill's pass reported no false finding (seventeen of seventeen and nineteen of nineteen checked in the tree). On the teammate's key the skill found five of seven primary items on #817 and two of seven on #778, the reviewer none on either; the five primary items both passes missed on #778 are the kind a reproduction finds rather than a read, which is the verifier's job on each candidate and the regression pass #73 keeps.

Two caveats recorded with the decision: on #778 the skill's fork read Redline's later fix titles at its fifth tool call, before opening any file, so three of its four overlaps with the reviewer are not independent there (the #817 overlaps are: that fork saw the titles after every finding's first touch), and the key itself was unexposed on both PRs; and the rule's "misses what the reviewer finds" was applied by cause, as the reviewer's own collapse rule reads it. Decided by: @michaelwitwicki, by the rule in issue #79 task 1, on §9's evidence, 2026-11-05.
